The Santa Grotto experience

More than a visit to Santa — every part of the experience is designed to delight your child and your family.

How it works

From booking to Christmas memories

Everything is handled online so there's no queue on the day — walk up, show your Golden Ticket, and step inside.

01 · BOOK

Pick a date & session

Choose a December date and a morning, afternoon or evening session at Auldon Toys, Lekki. If a session is full, join its waiting list.

02 · ARRIVE

Check in with your ticket

Show the QR code on your Golden Ticket at the grotto entrance — no queue, no paperwork.

03 · EXPERIENCE

Meet Santa

Your child meets Santa, listens to his Christmas story and captures Christmas photo moments inside the grotto.

04 · RECEIVE

A gift from Santa

Santa presents each child's gift — plus any Santa Gift Bundle or Gift Voucher you've added.

05 · DISCOVER

Explore & shop

Discover Christmas gifts, toys, hampers and seasonal offerings in store before you head home.
