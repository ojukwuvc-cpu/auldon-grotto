Privacy & booking terms

Last updated 26 October 2026. Questions about these terms? WhatsApp +234 810 002 5985.

Your booking

  • One Experience Ticket covers one family visit (up to 6 children) in the session you choose. Please arrive within your session.
  • Your place is held for 30 minutes while you pay. If the transfer doesn't arrive in that time the booking expires and no money is taken.
  • Tickets are non-refundable, but can be moved to another date or session subject to availability — WhatsApp us on +234 810 002 5985 with your booking reference.
  • Gift bundles contain toys and selected Christmas items; final contents depend on stock. Vouchers are redeemed in store up to their value.

Your privacy

  • We collect the details you enter here (parent name, WhatsApp number, email, and each child's first name, age, interests and letter to Santa) only to run your visit, send your Golden Ticket and contact you about your booking.
  • Payment is handled by Flutterwave; we never see your card or bank details.
  • We count visits to this page with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you.
  • Photos are only taken of children whose parent has given photo consent when booking.
  • We don't sell or share your details with anyone else, and we delete booking details after the season ends (by 31 March 2027).
  • To see, correct or delete your details, WhatsApp +234 810 002 5985 or email the address on your ticket. Auldon Toys follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act.
